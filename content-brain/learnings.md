## 2026-07-17 · Baseline (from Bamidele directly, not inferred)
- No em dashes under any circumstances. Replace with period, comma or colon.
- No question-based hooks. First line is always a statement.
- No jargon: leverage, streamline, unlock, empower, seamless, game-changer.
- Do not touch his rhythm when he edits. His phrasing wins even when it breaks a guideline.
- Nigerian-specific references over generic ones, but never forced.
- CTA style is the DM keyword ("DM me SCORECARD"), not "link in comments" gymnastics.
- Lead with proof, never aspiration. Okoh receipts open pitches; failed experiments (Insight Flow, Synk) are never the opener.
