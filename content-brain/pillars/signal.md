# Pillar: Signal (Wed hot take, Thu news + take)

**Job:** make Bamidele the interpreter of Nigerian business and ops news for SME owners. Authority between the proof posts.
**Reader:** same SME owner, plus the wider Nigerian LinkedIn business audience. This pillar travels furthest; keep it sharp.

## Wednesday format: the hot take
60 to 120 words. One contrarian or clarifying opinion, stated plainly, defended in three or four sentences. No research needed beyond conviction and one fact.
Standing takes to rotate and extend: Nigerian SMEs do not need AI yet, they need consolidation. Custom is not always better than off-the-shelf. The most expensive employee is the one holding the process in their head. Cheap tools are expensive when there are five of them.

## Thursday format: news + take
Structure: the news item in two sentences > why it matters to an SME owner in one sentence > the systems angle in three or four sentences > one practical implication.
Research inputs (morning run): Nairametrics, BusinessDay, NCDMB news, Punch business, TechCabal, NBS releases. Filter for: SME policy, oil and gas local content, procurement, digitization, funding for indigenous firms. Last 48 hours preferred, last 7 days acceptable.

## Hook patterns
- "NCDMB just [did thing]. Most owners will miss what it means."
- "Everyone is reading this news wrong."
- "[Fact from the news]. Here is the part that touches your operation."
- "Unpopular: [take]."

## CTA rule
None. Signal posts never sell. They earn the right to sell on other days.

## Quality bar
Good: a take nobody else in the feed is making, grounded in one verifiable fact, sourced in research/.
Bad: summarizing news without a position. If it reads like a newsletter, it fails.
