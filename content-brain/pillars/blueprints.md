# Pillar: Blueprints (Tue, flagship)

**Job:** demonstrate the audit method in public. Give away the thinking so completely that a professional could act on it, and a business owner realizes he would rather pay AlphaWGA to do it.
**Reader:** two at once. The SME owner in the niche being torn down. The skilled professional (developer, accountant, ops person) who could package this skill into an offer.

## Format: the niche teardown
Structure: pick one Nigerian niche > name its 3 biggest operational leaks (specific, costed where possible) > the blueprint: what a consolidated system looks like for that niche, tools and steps > what a professional could charge to fix it > one line on how AlphaWGA approaches it.
This is Tuesday. It is the longest post of the week. It should feel like the reader stole something valuable.

## Quality bar
Good: names real tools, real naira ranges, real workflow steps. The blueprint must be executable.
Bad: vague advice ("digitize your processes"). If a post could apply to any business, it fails.
