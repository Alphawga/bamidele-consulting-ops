## Draft A

Bible before work. The list before the code.

Every morning starts the same way before it starts being about AlphaWGA. Scripture first,
then the list for the day, then the work. Not because it makes me more productive. Because
it puts the day in order before the day tries to put me in order.

Some mornings I skip it. Those are always the days I answer messages faster and think
slower. Busy is not the same as clear.

Discipline is not the exciting part of building anything. It is the boring part that decides
whether the exciting part lasts.

Why this angle: opens with his own established hook line verbatim, low risk, strong fit for a first Saturday post since it is his most quoted personal line.

## Draft B

I teach 20 young people every Wednesday. They teach me back.

I go in thinking I am the one with answers. Systems, procurement, how to think about a
business problem. Then a 19 year old asks a question that has no clean answer, and I have to
actually think again instead of repeating something I already believe.

That is the trade. I give them structure. They give me back the habit of not assuming I have
already figured everything out.

Teaching is the cheapest way I know to stay honest about what I still do not understand.

Why this angle: names the specific NDI Wednesday commitment, warm without performance, principle lands on humility rather than generosity, avoids sounding like a humble-brag.

## Draft C

I mentor one young man named Fatai. Not twenty. One.

It is easier to show up for a room of twenty than for one person who calls you when things
go wrong at 11pm. The room forgives an off week. One person notices.

Fatai is not a case study. He is just someone I said yes to, and now I have to keep saying
yes to, on the weeks I do not feel like it too.

The size of who you show up for does not matter as much as whether you still show up when it
is inconvenient.

Why this angle: contrasts with the "20 young people" theme by narrowing to one named relationship, gives the post a distinct shape from Draft B so the two do not read as the same idea twice.
