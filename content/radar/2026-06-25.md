## 1. 88% of AI agents never reach production. The cause is the data, not the model.

**Hook:** Companies are building AI agents on top of disconnected systems. The agents fail before they scale. The model is not the problem.

**Context:** Multiple 2026 enterprise AI reports land on the same finding: almost 4 in 5 enterprises have AI agents deployed somewhere, but only 1 in 9 runs them in production. Pilot-to-production failure sits at 70 to 90 percent. The consistent root cause is fragmented, siloed data. An agent working with incomplete or disconnected data produces incomplete or disconnected output. Gartner expects more than 40 percent of agentic AI projects to be cancelled outright by end of 2027.

**His angle:** This is the one-system argument from the other direction. The AI layer is not what fails. The operations data underneath it is what fails. Unified, connected data is the prerequisite for any AI initiative, not something you add later. Companies spending on AI while their systems are still fragmented are learning this the expensive way.

**Suggested format:** LinkedIn post. High shareability, clear contrarian point, relevant across all sectors.

## 4. The CBN has not supplied FX to the market in six consecutive weeks. Procurement teams cannot anchor to a rate that no longer exists.

**Hook:** Six weeks of zero CBN FX supply. The Naira is entirely market-driven now. Procurement planning changes with it.

**Context:** Nigeria's FX market inflows dropped 26 percent as the CBN recorded six consecutive weeks of zero supply as of mid-June. Exporters now account for 43 percent of total FX supply, foreign portfolio investors 39 percent. The Naira closed at N1,370 per USD at the NFEM window. Gross reserves rose to $51 billion, suggesting the CBN is holding reserves rather than intervening in the market.

**His angle:** For procurement teams in oil and gas, this shifts planning assumptions in real time. MRO materials, capital equipment, and spare parts priced in USD now move against a market rate with no official anchor. Companies that track FX exposure inside their procurement systems catch the change early. Companies running it in spreadsheets discover it at month-end review.

**Context:** Bain's 2026 oil and gas M&A report shows the top 20 acquirers controlled 53 percent of all deal value over the past decade. The rest spend years paying for two parallel systems and wondering why the numbers do not improve.
