## 3. CBN has ordered every bank and fintech to store Nigerian payment data inside Nigeria by January 1, 2027.

**What happened.** The CBN issued a directive in June 2026 requiring all payment and
financial transaction data generated in Nigeria to be processed, stored, and managed on
servers inside Nigeria. Deadline: January 1, 2027. Banks, fintechs, mobile money operators,
and payment service providers are all covered. Most currently run on AWS or Azure
infrastructure hosted outside Nigeria. Data centres are heavily concentrated in Lagos,
which the CBN's own commentary flags as a systemic risk.

**His angle.** This is a systems-architecture mandate dressed as a compliance rule. Every
company running a Nigerian business through a SaaS stack hosted abroad now needs to audit
where their financial data actually lives. Companies with fragmented systems across five
different tools for payments, invoicing, and reconciliation will have the hardest time
proving compliance. The company with one integrated system audits once and is done. This
is one of the clearest external arguments for consolidation that has appeared this year.

## 6. MIT: Agentic AI has succeeded where coding succeeded. Everything else is still hard.

**What happened.** On June 30, MIT News published a Q&A with computer scientist Phillip
Isola on agentic AI. His direct assessment: "The area where we've seen the most success has
been with coding agents." The reason is data. There is abundant training data showing how
humans solve coding problems step by step. Booking a flight or routing an invoice through
three departments does not have that data yet. An agent starts with a generative AI core,
then companies add tools and wrappers. Without the right data, the agent cannot learn the
right steps.

**His angle.** Most agentic AI excitement is running ahead of actual results. Coding agents
work because the process is well-documented, at scale, with clear right and wrong outcomes.
For a business owner, the practical read is this: AI agents are reliable inside
well-defined, well-documented workflows. If your process is not written down anywhere, an
agent will not fix it. Your documentation is your training data. Fixing your operations
on paper is step one, not step two.

**Source.** MIT News, June 30: https://news.mit.edu/2026/agentic-ai-and-what-do-we-want-it-be-0630
