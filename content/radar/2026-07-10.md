## 4. The agentic AI standard has shifted: "on the loop," not "in the loop"

**Hook:** In 2025 we talked about keeping humans in the loop. In 2026 the standard is humans on the loop. The difference matters for anyone building AI into their operations.

**Context:** Enterprise AI systems now coordinate networks of specialist agents: a planner, an executor, a compliance checker, a verifier, all sharing state across one operation. Humans supervise from above and intervene at defined decision checkpoints rather than approving each action. Products launching in July 2026 across HR, lending, procurement, and product data management reflect this architecture. 79% of companies report AI agent adoption. Only 66% have seen measurable gains.

**His angle:** The "on the loop" model only holds if your business processes are documented clearly enough that the system knows where a decision point is. That documentation problem is an operations problem before it becomes an AI problem. If you cannot tell me at what step a purchase order requires a second approval, or why, the agent will not figure it out either.

## 6. Oil and gas MRO: integrated systems show 15 to 30% cost reduction

**Hook:** The global MRO market for oil and gas was valued at $68 billion in 2025. Companies combining IoT monitoring, real-time inventory, and e-procurement platforms are cutting total MRO costs by 15 to 30% against traditional reactive approaches, per 2026 industry analysis.

**Context:** The MRO market is projected to reach $112.7 billion by 2034. The companies widening their cost advantage are not doing it through better supplier contracts alone. They are connecting maintenance data to procurement data to inventory data across sites. Digital supply chain integration is the differentiator, not individual tool upgrades.

**His angle:** For Nigerian oil and gas operators, MRO is often the highest source of procurement leakage. Parts are ordered reactively. The 15 to 30% reduction applies to companies with integrated systems. Without integration, there is nothing to optimise.
