## 6. Nigeria loses roughly 30% of procurement resources to fraud

**Hook:** 30% of Nigeria's procurement resources are lost to fraud. Most of it hides in the gaps between systems.

**Context:** The Chartered Institute of Purchasing and Supply Management of Nigeria published research showing Nigeria loses approximately 30% of its procurement resources to fraud. The most common schemes are phantom vendors, fake invoices, and over-invoicing. These succeed in environments where purchase orders, delivery confirmations, and payment approvals live in different places with no automatic cross-check between them.

**Your angle:** Procurement fraud is not just a problem of dishonest people. It is a problem of gaps. The gap between what was ordered and what arrived. The gap between approval and payment. When those steps happen in different systems, or different people's inboxes, the gap is easy to work. One connected procurement record that ties the order to the receipt to the payment closes most hiding places without adding a governance layer.

**Format:** Article or LinkedIn post

**Source:** https://www.publicprocurement.ng/nigeria-losing-30-of-its-resources-to-procurement-fraud-institute
