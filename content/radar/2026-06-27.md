## 5. 89 percent of executives say AI had zero impact on their productivity

**Published:** May 27, 2026 (Fortune)  
**Suggested format:** LinkedIn article  
**Source:** https://fortune.com/2026/05/27/ai-productivity-internet-boom-solow-paradox/

**Hook:** A major NBER study found 89 percent of executives saw no productivity change from AI adoption. Economists are calling it the Solow Paradox: the same gap that appeared when computers first arrived in offices 40 years ago.

**Context:** Research from the National Bureau of Economic Research spanning the US, UK, Germany, and Australia found 90% of managers report zero impact on employment over three years and 89% saw no productivity change. Fortune noted that nearly 40% of productivity gains from AI are lost to rework. The pattern mirrors Robert Solow's 1987 observation: "You can see the computer age everywhere except in the productivity statistics."

**Your angle:** You cannot automate a mess. AI tools dropped onto disconnected systems just move the friction around. The procurement and operations teams getting measurable gains from AI are the ones who connected their data first, then added intelligence on top. The sequence matters. Connected system first. Automation second.
