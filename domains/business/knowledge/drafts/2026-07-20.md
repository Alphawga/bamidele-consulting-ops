# PM — no CTA (calendar rule, applies even though AM already has one today)
Angle: Input feels like progress. It isn't.

## Draft A

Input feels like progress. It isn't.

I have one LinkedIn post published, ever. I have fifteen ideas sitting in a backlog file,
untouched. This week I read a whole book and told myself I was "doing the work."

I was not. I was doing the thing that feels like the work without the risk of the work.

It is the same mistake I point out in other people's businesses. Adding a new tool feels like
progress. Using the one you already have is the actual progress.

Why this angle: closest to the raw backlog seed, most direct and specific, strongest
"honest number" hook for a Monday evening slot right after a Proof post about someone else's
business.

## Draft B

I told a client this week: stop adding tools, use the one you have.

Then I looked at my own notes. Fifteen post ideas. One published post.

Reading a third book on content strategy felt like work. It was not. It was avoiding the one
thing that actually counts, which is publishing the first one.

Same lesson, different business. Mine.

Why this angle: frames the admission as something surfaced by advising a client first, then
turned back on himself, slightly softer opener than Draft A, may read as more self-aware and
less like a confession out of nowhere.
