# Pillar: Person (Sat)

**Job:** none, commercially. This is Bamidele the man: faith, discipline, the N35M journey, teaching 20+ young people at NDI, mentoring Fatai. It is why strangers trust the other six days.
**Reader:** everyone. These posts travel beyond the niche and that is fine.

## Format
Structure: one true moment from the week or the past > what it cost or taught > the principle, stated without preaching. Short to medium. Warm, plain, no performance.
Standing themes: Bible before work, the list before the code. Building in Lagos with NEPA and traffic as coworkers. What teaching youth teaches the teacher. Discipline as the boring superpower. The distance between current income and the goal, told honestly.

## Hard rules
- No CTA. Ever. Not even a P.S.
- No business pitch dressed as a life lesson. If the post secretly sells, it fails.
- Faith content is testimony, not sermon. One person's practice, offered plainly.
- Vulnerability yes, self-pity no. Every hard thing lands on a principle.

## Hook patterns
- "Bible before work. The list before the code."
- "I teach 20 young people every Wednesday. They teach me back."
- "Lagos gave me two coworkers I never hired: traffic and NEPA."
- "My income does not match my skills yet. Here is what I do about that."

## Quality bar
Good: specific, humble, one clear principle.
Bad: motivational wallpaper. If it could be a stock quote image, it fails.
