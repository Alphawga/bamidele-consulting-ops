# Daniel Priestley: How to Become Famous in Your Industry (and Make $1 Million)

## Core thesis

Income in every industry follows a power law, not a bell curve. Most people earn 50K to 150K; the top few earn exponentially more. The top are "Key People of Influence" (KPIs). Becoming one is not about more functional skill. It is about five new skills that most workers never develop: pitching, publishing, product, profile, and partnerships.

## The industry ladder and the merry-go-round of distraction

Every industry has three levels:

- **Newbies**: learning, excited, low skill. Currency is learning.
- **Workers**: skilled, paid, but stuck in the 50K to 150K band. Often unhappy.
- **Key People of Influence**: well-known, well-connected, highly valued. Earn exponentially more.

The trap is the **merry-go-round of distraction**: a worker in industry A sees a KPI in industry B, jumps over as a newbie, works up to worker status, gets disappointed, then jumps to industry C. Each reset kills momentum. The fix is to choose one industry you love and move from worker to KPI inside it.

## The five skills

### 1. Pitching

The ability to enroll people in new ideas. "You get what you pitch for and you're always pitching." Three types:

- **Social pitch**: 30 to 45 seconds. Covers name, same, fame, aim, and game.
- **Scheduled pitch**: 15 minutes to 2 hours. Tailored presentation that moves someone from path A to path B.
- **Sales pitch**: transactional. Gets someone to buy or invest.

### 2. Publishing

Make your ideas public. The word "author" is inside "authority" for a reason. Prolific beats perfect. Be a directionalist, not a perfectionist. Publish videos, podcasts, social posts, and books. The Beatles wrote ~400 songs and only 30 to 40 became number ones; the hits carry the rest.

### 3. Product

Do not sell time for money. Build a **product ecosystem** with four types:

- **Gift**: free, generates attention.
- **Product for prospects**: cheap, low-commitment, builds trust.
- **Core offering**: the main transformation, the flagship.
- **Product for clients**: ongoing subscription, membership, or continuation offer.

Income follows assets. Product creation is one of the highest-value assets today. Products and services don't make money alone; product and service ecosystems do.

### 4. Profile

How you are seen from a distance. Use the **SALT** framework:

- **S**ocial media accounts: show up regularly on at least four platforms.
- **A**wards and associations: win awards and attach to known brands.
- **L**ive events: speak, host, sponsor.
- **T**hird-party platforms: appear on other people's podcasts, media, and stages.

Every opportunity stands behind a Google search. "You are who Google says you are." Profile is not about narcissism or chasing the spotlight. It is about becoming the spotlight: "Look at this idea," not "Look at me." You also get famous by the success of your clients.

### 5. Partnerships

Only KPIs can do partnerships. Three types:

- **Brand partnerships**: attach to someone more well-known.
- **Product partnerships**: bundle complementary products.
- **Distribution partnerships**: get your product in front of more people.

Great partnerships should produce 1 + 1 = 11. Someone already has everything you want: money, expertise, audience. They are looking for a partnership; your job is to structure it.
