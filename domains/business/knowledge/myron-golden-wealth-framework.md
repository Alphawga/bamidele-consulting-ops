# Myron Golden: From zero to millionaire — distilled notes

## Core thesis

Income follows assets. If you don't like your income, don't evaluate the income. Evaluate the assets that income is following. You are the resource; the asset is the thing you produce for other people that keeps paying you.

## Wealth formula

**Mindset + skill set + tool set = assets.** Income follows assets.

You cannot skip the order of being → doing → having. Most people want the property (having) without the identity (being) and activity (doing). That is why they stay frustrated.

## Mindset shifts required

- Wealth is not morally wrong. Money is not the primary benefit of work; it is the byproduct. The real benefits are skills, relationships, and lessons learned.
- Money gives options to do more good for the people and causes you care about.
- The primary purpose of money is not to buy things. It is to turn into more money. Make it faster, hold it longer, multiply it while holding it, and spend it slower.
- Do a "wealth study": study how poor people think vs. rich people think, what they do, and what they accumulate. Poor people accumulate entertainment; rich people accumulate knowledge.

## How to discover value

Value is discovered by asking questions and caring about the answers. Stop trying to be more interesting; be more interested.

Where value comes from:

1. **Past perceived voids** create present pursued values. (What do people feel was missing?)
2. **Past perceived violations** create present pursued virtues. (What do people want to protect themselves from?)
3. **Present perceived virtues** create present perceived values. (What do they already see as good?)
4. **Future perceived visions** create present pursued values. (What do they want to become?)

For Bamidele, Nigerian procurement and oil-and-gas service firms have a void: scattered operations, QuickBooks plus WhatsApp plus manual reminders, no single source of truth. That void is where his consolidation offer lives.

## How to describe the value you create

The transformation must be:

- **Measurable:** clear before and after. e.g., "from scattered tools to one system" or "from manual follow-up to automated reminders."
- **Desirable:** they must already want it, not what you want them to want.
- **Believable:** you must believe you can deliver it, and you must look the part.
- **Statable in a sound bite:** no jargon, no word salad. Plain English.

This maps directly to Bamidele's core line: "I consolidate scattered business operations into one intelligent system." It is measurable, desirable for the right buyer, and statable.

## The content-audience-brand-offer sequence

1. Create content (a deep pool of it).
2. Attract an audience.
3. Build a personal brand.
4. Offer your offers.

Personal brand means becoming the person people say yes to before they even know the offer. The number one reason people say yes or no is who brought the offer, not price or product.

### The 7-11-4 rule (Daniel Priestley / Robin Dunbar)

People need to spend **7 hours** with you, have **11 interactions**, across **4 platforms** before they feel they know, like, and trust you enough to buy.

For Bamidele's platforms: LinkedIn, YouTube, website/blog, and one more (email or newsletter). The goal is to create enough content that someone could "binge" him for seven hours.

## The offer ladder

You can move zeros to increase income without increasing work proportionally:

- $20 book / small product → 50,000 sales = $1M
- $200 product → 5,000 sales = $1M
- $2,000 course / program → 500 sales = $1M
- $20,000 mastermind → 50 sales = $1M
- $100,000+ high-ticket → 10 sales = $1M

For Bamidele, the ladder could be:

- Low: $5K–$10K diagnostic / audit call (paid qualification)
- Mid: ₦200K–₦500K/month procurement retainer (the cash-flow engine)
- High: custom ERP build or partnership / co-ownership deal
- Very high: mastermind or equity play for oil-and-gas procurement operators

## Key operating principles to apply

1. **Build assets that pay more than once.** The Okoh ERP is an asset. SkinAI is an asset. The website, content, and personal brand are assets. The retainer relationship is an asset. Treat them that way.
2. **Content is the pool people wait in before they meet you.** Bamidele should create enough LinkedIn/YouTube content that a prospect can binge him for hours.
3. **Community service content ratio:** at least 10 pieces of pure service content for every 1 promotional post. This builds trust before the ask.
4. **Affiliate path:** if you don't have an offer yet, sell someone else's offer that aligns with your audience. Bamidele already has offers; this is less relevant now, but useful for future courses or tools.
5. **Selling is not convincing.** It is helping someone make a decision they already desire to make for their own reasons. The diagnostic call is the perfect format for this.
6. **You are already being paid in proportion to the value you deliver.** To earn more, deliver higher value to more people in less time. The consolidation thesis does this: one system replaces many tools and many manual hours.
