# AlphaWGA content framework

A repeatable system for deciding what to publish and how to publish it, so you do not have to reinvent the wheel every week. Built from the AlphaWGA voice (`alphawga-voice.md`), the four content pillars, and inspiration from operators like Layla Pomper at ProcessDriven.

## Content philosophy

- **One idea, one post.** No listicles. No "7 things you must know." One sharp angle per post.
- **Concrete over abstract.** Every post must include a real scene, a real number, a real name, or a real client story.
- **Nigerian context.** Lagos, Naira, oil and gas, procurement, fintech, SMEs. Not generic advice with local names swapped in.
- **Value first, pitch last.** Some posts sell nothing on purpose. The ones that sell use a single P.S. line.
- **Repurpose everything.** One long-form idea becomes a LinkedIn post, a blog post, a short video, and a carousels thread.

## The four pillars

| Pillar | Purpose | What it sounds like | Example angle |
|--------|---------|---------------------|---------------|
| **Proof** | Show that you have done this before. | Case study, client story, build log. | "We retired five tools in one quarter." |
| **Blueprints** | Teach how to solve the problem. | Framework, methodology, step-by-step. | "The three-system audit that finds operational leaks." |
| **Signal** | React to what is happening in the market. | News, trend, hot take, regulatory shift. | "NCDMB is auditing contractors in Q3." |
| **Person** | Show the human behind the brand. | Personal story, behind the scenes, values. | "Bible before work, the list before the code." |

## Content types

### 1. Case study story (Proof)

A real client or project, told as a before/after scene. The client is the hero. You are the guide.

- Open with the specific moment the problem became visible.
- Show the mess before the fix.
- Show the one decision that changed the trajectory.
- Close with the result, or mark `[TO FILL]` if the number is not verified.

**Frequency:** once per week, usually Friday or Monday.
**Format:** LinkedIn post + blog post + potential case-study page on the site.
**CTA:** P.S. DM me SCORECARD.

### 2. Trending discussion (Signal)

React to news, regulation, or market movement in your space. Do not just summarize. Connect it to the AlphaWGA thesis.

- State the news in one sentence.
- Explain why it matters to Nigerian operators.
- Connect it to consolidation, records, or systems.
- Close with a takeaway or prediction.

**Frequency:** once per week, usually Wednesday or Thursday.
**Format:** LinkedIn post + thread of 2-3 follow-up posts.
**Sources:** content radar, daily news, client conversations.

### 3. Value framework (Blueprints)

Teach a method or mental model. This is the "how you think" content that builds authority.

- Name the framework.
- Explain the one problem it solves.
- Give 3-5 steps or principles.
- Show where it came from (client work, your own experience, a book you read).

**Frequency:** once per week, usually Tuesday.
**Format:** LinkedIn post + blog post + carousel/short video.
**CTA:** P.S. the SCORECARD is the first step.

### 4. Labour Market Blueprint (Blueprints — Tuesday series)

A recurring Tuesday series for Nigerian professionals who want to stop sending CVs and start using value in a domain. Each post gives a clear blueprint with examples and references for one domain or skill area.

- Name the domain or skill.
- Show what "sending CVs" looks like and why it fails.
- Give a concrete alternative: a project, a portfolio piece, a free audit, a small paid offer.
- Include real examples or references when possible.

**Frequency:** every Tuesday.
**Format:** LinkedIn post + blog post + potential newsletter/lead magnet.
**Working title:** "Tuesday Blueprint."
**Example topics:**
- Operations analyst: build a three-system audit for a local business.
- Procurement officer: document one vendor onboarding process.
- Developer: ship one small tool that solves a business problem.
- Data analyst: clean and visualize one public dataset.
- Sales: record five discovery calls and find the pattern.

### 5. Personal / behind the scenes (Person)

The human side of building AlphaWGA. Not motivational. Specific scenes and decisions.

- One moment, one lesson, one adjustment.
- Connect personal habit to business outcome.
- No pitch, or a very soft one.

**Frequency:** once per week, usually Saturday.
**Format:** LinkedIn post only.

## Weekly content rhythm

| Day | AM post | Pillar | PM post | Pillar |
|-----|---------|--------|---------|--------|
| Monday | Case study or proof story | Proof | Short observation or admission | Person/Signal |
| Tuesday | **Labour Market Blueprint** | Blueprints | Repurpose/clip from Monday/Tuesday | — |
| Wednesday | Value framework or how-to | Blueprints | Short take on a trend | Signal |
| Thursday | Trending discussion | Signal | Quick tip or personal note | Person/Blueprints |
| Friday | Build log or case study close | Proof | Community signal or question | Person |
| Saturday | Personal story | Person | Rest or repurpose | — |
| Sunday | Week recap + SCORECARD CTA | Recap | Forward-looking note | — |

## Repurposing workflow

From one core idea per week, produce:

1. **LinkedIn AM post** (long-form text, 200-400 words).
2. **LinkedIn PM post** (short take, 50-100 words, no CTA).
3. **Blog post** (500-800 words) for `~/bamidele-consulting/content/blog/`.
4. **Short video script** (60-90 seconds) for future YouTube/Instagram/TikTok.
5. **Carousel outline** (5-7 slides) for Instagram/LinkedIn document posts.
6. **Email or newsletter version** when the email list exists.

## Voice check before publishing

Read the post aloud. If it could have been written by ChatGPT, rewrite it. Ask:

- Is there a real scene or number?
- Is there a Nigerian context?
- Would Bamidele actually say this sentence out loud?
- Does it end with a clear takeaway?
- Is the CTA limited to one P.S. line?

## Content idea sources

1. **Content radar PR** — merged into `domains/business/content/radar/`. Strongest picks go to `domains/business/content/backlog.md`.
2. **Client conversations** — every client interaction is a potential Proof or Blueprint post.
3. **Your own operations** — AlphaBrain, finance tracker, daily rhythm are all Person/Blueprint material.
4. **Books you read** — distill frameworks into Blueprint posts.
5. **Competitors and peers** — Layla Pomper, ProcessDriven, and others doing operations consulting well. Study their formats, not their voice.

## What to track

- **Reach per post** (not just likes).
- **Profile visits** from LinkedIn.
- **SCORECARD completions** and source attribution.
- **Diagnostic calls booked** from LinkedIn vs. outreach vs. referrals.
- **Which pillar performs best** for leads vs. engagement.

Review every Sunday. Double down on what works. Kill what does not.
