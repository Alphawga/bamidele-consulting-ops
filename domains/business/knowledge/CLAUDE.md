## Voice rules (non-negotiable)

- No em dashes. Ever. Use a period, comma or colon instead.
- No jargon: leverage, streamline, unlock, empower, seamless, game-changer, delve.
- No question hooks. The first line is a statement.
- Short, direct sentences. Storytelling first, insight second, CTA last and only on allowed days.
- Nigerian-specific texture where natural: naira, WhatsApp, Lagos, procurement. Never forced.
- When Bamidele edits a draft, his phrasing wins. Do not smooth his rhythm back out. Log the edit instead.

## LinkedIn format rules

- 150 to 300 words. Wednesday can run 60 to 120.
- Hook line under 12 words. It must stand alone before the fold.
- One idea per post. White space every one or two sentences.
- Zero to three hashtags, end of post, niche-specific. Default zero.
- One CTA maximum. Style: DM keyword ("DM me SCORECARD") or one plain link.

## The learning loop (protocol, never skip)

1. **Before drafting:** read learnings.md in full, today's pillar file, and the last 7 entries in published/.
2. **Draft:** write 3 options into drafts/YYYY-MM-DD.md. Different hooks and angles, same pillar. Each carries a one-line "why this angle".
3. **After publishing:** Bamidele runs /learn with his final version. Diff it against the chosen draft. Append a dated entry to learnings.md: what he changed, the rule you infer from it.
4. **Promotion:** any rule that appears twice in learnings.md gets promoted into this file or the relevant pillar file. Say so when you do it.
5. **Weekly:** Sunday, /weekly with engagement numbers. Update each pillar's "what worked" log and write next week's angles into calendar.md.

- Never fabricate numbers, clients or stories. If a Proof post needs a number you do not have, ask.

Do not "fix" the no-CTA rule by pointing to how bigger creators do it.
