### Wednesday 22 — Signal / AM

**Nigerian SMEs do not need AI yet.**

They need consolidation. AI cannot fix what has never been recorded, and most Nigerian businesses are still running on WhatsApp chats, one Excel sheet, and one person who knows where everything is.

I am not anti-AI. I am pro-foundation. The companies that will win with AI are the ones that built the record first. Purchase history. Inventory patterns. Delivery records. Customer quotes.

Without that, AI is just a faster way to make bad decisions.

Build the record first. The intelligence comes after.

*P.S. The SCORECARD tells you whether your operation is ready for AI or still ready for consolidation.*

### Saturday 25 — PM

**I stopped sorting source material before I understood it.**

Everything used to go into a category at capture. The problem was, I often did not know the right category yet. I was making a human sort a pile of things the system did not understand.

Now everything drops into one raw inbox. It gets classified at the distillation step, after I have seen the pattern.

Same fix I hand clients drowning in miscategorized inventory or receipts. Capture first. Classify with intelligence downstream. Do not make the human sort blind.
