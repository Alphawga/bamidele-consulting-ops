## 1. Bank-feed transaction matching + auto-posting engine

- **What it is:** Plaid-connected bank feed, rule-based matcher, GL posting, and an exclude
  flow — the reconcile-a-bank-statement loop, automated.
- **Source:** Okoh ERP (`src/lib/accounting/bank-sync/`) · **client-engagement**
- **Market read:** This is one of the most commoditized features in accounting software.
  Xero's bank-rules engine and QuickBooks Online both ship this natively for SMBs
  ([Bench roundup](https://www.bench.co/blog/bookkeeping/9-bookkeeping-tools-that-auto-categorize-bank-expenses),
  [Airwallex roundup](https://www.airwallex.com/en-us/blog/best-automated-reconciliation-software-solutions)).
  At the enterprise end, HighRadius and Trintech's Adra Matcher do the same thing with
  higher-volume multi-way matching ([HighRadius](https://www.highradius.com/resources/Blog/best-bank-reconciliation-tools/),
  [Numeric](https://www.numeric.io/blog/account-reconciliation-software)). On the open-source
  side, [Blnk](https://github.com/blnkfinance/blnk) is an active open-source ledger core that
  already does rule-based reconciliation against bank/processor records, and smaller
  single-purpose tools exist too (`plaid2text`, `plaid-sync`, `bankstatementparser` —
  connector and parser utilities, not full matching engines). I could not find evidence of a
  standalone, sellable "matching engine" product distinct from either full accounting
  platforms or Blnk's open-source core — I'm flagging that as unverified rather than
  asserting a gap that isn't there.
- **Gap, if any:** Not at the SMB tier — Xero/QBO already give this away as a feature, so
  nobody pays separately for it. The narrower gap is emerging-market bank connectivity:
  Plaid doesn't cover Nigeria, so an Okoh-style engine already had to be built generically
  enough to swap in a local aggregator. A connector-agnostic matching/rules core (in the
  spirit of Blnk, but reusable by any local ERP) is a more plausible open-source play than
  a paid product.
- **Recommendation:** Open-source, not sell — but only the generic rules/matching core, and
  only once Alpha clears extraction with the Okoh client. As a closed product it's competing
  with free, bundled features in Xero/QBO and a funded open-source project (Blnk); as an
  open-source contribution decoupled from any specific bank connector, it's a credible
  dev-rel/portfolio piece for Bamidele Consulting. This stays conditional either way — it's
  client-engagement work, not owned IP.

## 2. Idempotent GRN-to-GL posting engine (GRNI / three-way match)

- **What it is:** Posts goods-received-not-invoiced (GRNI) entries — Dr Inventory / Cr GRNI
  at receipt, cleared when the vendor bill lands — idempotently across two creation paths
  reconciled to one ledger effect.
- **Source:** Okoh ERP (`src/lib/accounting/posting-engines.ts`, GRN flow) · **client-engagement**
- **Market read:** GR/IR (goods-received-not-invoiced) three-way matching is a standard,
  well-documented ERP pattern — Odoo and ERPNext both implement automatic GRNI clearing
  natively for stockable products
  ([Odoo forum thread](https://www.odoo.com/forum/help-1/entreprise-v18-grni-goods-received-not-invoiced-accounting-entries-at-receipt-and-vendor-bill-in-odoo-294359),
  [Kadima Group writeup](https://kadimagroup.ca/odoo-goods-received-not-invoiced/)), and
  Stampli and other AP tools document the same GR/IR reconciliation problem at the
  enterprise end ([Stampli](https://www.stampli.com/resources/grir-reconciliation/)). So this
  isn't a novel idea — full ERPs already solve it.
- **Gap, if any:** The real gap is one tier down. SMB-focused tools that small businesses
  actually use (QuickBooks Online, Xero, Wave) don't do real three-way GRN-to-GL matching —
  that logic only shows up once you're on a full ERP. Nigerian/African SMB accounting tools
  in particular skew toward basic bookkeeping and bank reconciliation
  ([Angle 360 roundup](https://angle360ng.com/accounting-software-for-small-and-large-organisations-in-nigeria/),
  [MarkWide Research market sizing](https://markwideresearch.com/africa-accounting-budgeting-software-market))
  rather than inventory-to-ledger correctness, and the market research doesn't surface any
  African SMB tool naming GRNI/three-way match as a differentiator. That's a real, if narrow,
  gap — but it's a correctness feature, not something end users search for or pay a
  standalone fee on top of their accounting software for.
- **Recommendation:** License as a module/IP component, not open-source — again, only once
  Alpha resolves rights with Okoh. This is more defensible than #1: it's a deeper technical
  correctness problem that SMB-tier tools skip and full ERPs bury inside a much bigger
  product, so it's a plausible licensable add-on for another small-inventory-tool builder
  rather than something to give away for free (open-source doesn't monetize an accounting
  correctness fix, and the buyer here is a handful of other software builders, not end
  users). Still gated on the ownership question — nothing here is cleared to extract.
