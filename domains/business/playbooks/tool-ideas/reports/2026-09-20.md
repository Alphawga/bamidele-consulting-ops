## Skipped: bank-feed transaction matching + auto-posting engine

`2026-07-14 · Okoh ERP (src/lib/accounting/bank-sync/) · client-engagement · Bank-feed
transaction matching + auto-posting engine (Plaid connect, rule-based matcher, GL posting,
exclude flow)`

Skipping this one. Two problems stack up:

1. The core feature (bank rules that auto-categorize and match transactions) already ships
   free inside QuickBooks Online and Xero — the exact SMB market this would target already
   has it built in. ([QuickBooks bank rules](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/help-article/banking/set-bank-rules-categorize-online-banking-online/L0mjJl0nD_US_en_US),
   [Xero categorization](https://bankreconciler.app/blogCategoriseTransactions))
2. Plaid's own coverage docs list North America, UK, and Europe — Nigeria isn't in that
   list. ([Plaid global coverage](https://plaid.com/global/)) If the Okoh build actually
   targets Nigerian SMBs, a Plaid-based connector may not even reach the banks that matter
   there. I couldn't confirm Nigerian bank coverage either way from what's public — that's
   a real unknown, not a guess in either direction, and would need checking against Plaid's
   institution list directly before building anything on it.

At the high end, this space is also crowded and getting more automated: BlackLine,
Trintech, HighRadius, Numeric, and Ramp all sell reconciliation with 90-99%+ auto-match
rates. ([HighRadius roundup](https://www.highradius.com/resources/Blog/best-bank-reconciliation-tools/),
[Bluecopa roundup](https://www.bluecopa.com/blog/transaction-matching-softwares)) There's
no gap between "already free in QBO/Xero" and "already sold by five enterprise vendors."
Not worth researching further this cycle.

## Recommended: GRN-to-GL posting engine (GR/IR clearing)

`2026-07-14 · Okoh ERP (src/lib/accounting/posting-engines.ts, GRN flow) · client-engagement
· Idempotent GRN-to-GL posting engine (Dr Inventory / Cr GRNI, two creation paths
reconciled to one ledger effect)`

**What it is:** a posting engine that takes a goods-received-note event (regardless of
which of two paths created it) and posts a single, idempotent Dr Inventory / Cr GRNI
(goods-received-not-invoiced) entry, then clears it correctly when the vendor bill lands.
No duplicate postings, no orphaned GR/IR balances.

**Does this exist already?** Partially, and only inside full ERPs. Odoo has three-way
matching (PO / receipt / bill) but it flags mismatches rather than auto-posting through
them — a human still resolves exceptions.
([Odoo docs](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/13.0/applications/inventory_and_mrp/purchase/purchases/rfq/3_way_matching.html))
Oracle and Dynamics have receipt-accrual accounting built into their much larger platforms.
([Oracle receipt accrual](https://docs.oracle.com/cd/E13228_01/fscm9pbr0/eng/psbooks/spog/htm/spog48.htm),
[RSM/Dynamics posting profiles](https://technologyblog.rsmus.com/technologies/microsoft/dynamics-365-for-finance-and-supply-chain/automated-accounting-part-1-inventory-posting-profiles-purchase-orders/))
I found no standalone library or API that just does GR/IR posting — every implementation
I could verify is bundled inside a full ERP module, not sold or open-sourced separately.
That's a real gap, not just an unverified absence — I looked for a dedicated product and
came up empty, which is different from confirming one doesn't exist anywhere.

**Who'd want it:** builders of lightweight accounting/inventory tools (indie SaaS,
vertical ERPs for markets Odoo/NetSuite under-serve, e.g. African SMB tooling) who need
correct GR/IR handling but don't want to build or license a full ERP module for it. The
buyer is a developer team, not an end business — this is infrastructure, not a
user-facing product.

**Recommendation: sell-as-IP or open-source (not build a product), conditional on Okoh
rights clearance.**

This reads better as a small, well-tested library/API than a SaaS — the market is other
builders, not accountants, and there's no distribution advantage to owning a UI around
it. Open-sourcing it would build credibility for Bamidele Consulting (a working, tested
piece of accounting logic in public) at the cost of giving away the IP; selling it
(licensing to a vertical ERP vendor, or as a paid module) keeps the value but needs a buyer
lined up. Either path starts the same way: **this was built under the Okoh engagement, so
it is not Alpha's to extract or publish until the contract and client relationship confirm
the rights.** Nothing here changes that. If Alpha clears it with Okoh, open-sourcing is the
lower-risk first move to test interest before looking for a buyer.
