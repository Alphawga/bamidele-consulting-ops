## 1. Idempotent GRN-to-GL posting engine

- **What it is**: the goods-receipt-to-ledger posting logic from Okoh ERP — two creation
  paths (GRN entry, invoice match) reconciled to a single, idempotent GL effect
  (Dr Inventory / Cr GRNI at receipt, cleared against AP on invoice match).
- **Source**: Okoh ERP, `src/lib/accounting/posting-engines.ts` (GRN flow) · **client-engagement**
- **Market read**: GRNI (goods-received-not-invoiced) reconciliation is a well-known,
  named problem — SAP calls it GR/IR. Existing tooling is enterprise AP-automation:
  [Stampli's GR/IR reconciliation product](https://www.stampli.com/resources/grir-reconciliation/),
  [PLANERGY](https://planergy.com/blog/grni-reconciliation-process-benefits/), and
  [openecx](https://www.openecx.com/news-resources/grni-explained) all sell this as a
  feature aimed at mid-size and larger orgs with real AP teams. On the open-source side,
  full ERPs — [ERPNext](https://frappe.io/erpnext/open-source-accounting),
  [LedgerSMB](https://ledgersmb.org/), FrontAccounting — post GRN-to-GL automatically, but
  only as one piece of a whole ERP you'd have to adopt wholesale to get it.
  I did not find a standalone, drop-in GRNI-matching engine aimed at small businesses
  running lighter accounting stacks (QuickBooks, Xero) rather than a full ERP — that's a
  real gap, not a saturated one. Whether small businesses would actually pay for it on its
  own, versus just tolerating manual GRNI reconciliation as they do today, is the open
  question — I found no data on willingness to pay for a narrow tool like this, so treat
  that part as a guess, not a finding.
- **Recommendation**: worth a build-vs-sell conversation once rights are clear. This is
  IP is narrow enough to stand alone (a matching/posting library, not a full ERP module)
  and the gap looks real. **Conditional on Alpha and Okoh resolving what Bamidele Consulting
  is allowed to extract from the engagement** — this is client work, not owned code, and
  that has to be settled before anything here moves.

## 2. Bank-feed transaction matching + auto-posting engine

- **What it is**: Plaid-connected bank-feed ingestion, rule-based transaction matcher,
  GL auto-posting, and an exclude flow — from Okoh ERP's bank reconciliation module.
- **Source**: Okoh ERP, `src/lib/accounting/bank-sync/` · **client-engagement**
- **Market read**: this space is saturated at every tier. Small-business end:
  QuickBooks Online and Xero both ship built-in bank-feed matching. Mid-market:
  [Ramp](https://ramp.com/blog/accounting-reconciliation-software),
  [Tesorio](https://www.tesorio.com/blog/best-bank-reconciliation-software-in-2026-find-the-right-fit-before-you-buy),
  FloQast. Enterprise: [HighRadius](https://www.highradius.com/resources/Blog/best-bank-reconciliation-tools/)
  (claims ~90% auto-match rate), BlackLine, Ledge. Plaid itself is the standard
  connectivity layer most of these already build on, so "connects via Plaid" isn't
  differentiation on its own.
- **Recommendation**: skip. Not a build or sell candidate as a standalone product — the
  market is already crowded from QuickBooks up to enterprise, and a client-derived engine
  wouldn't out-compete tools already at 90%+ match rates. It stays useful as internal Okoh
  ERP plumbing, nothing more, and that's fine.
