## 1. Idempotent GRN-to-GL posting engine

**What it is:** Okoh ERP's goods-received-note-to-general-ledger posting engine
(`src/lib/accounting/posting-engines.ts`, GRN flow). Two different flows can create a GRN, but
both reconcile to one ledger effect (Dr Inventory / Cr GRNI) — no double-posting regardless of
entry path.

**Source repo + ownership:** Okoh ERP (`src/lib/accounting/posting-engines.ts`, GRN flow) ·
`client-engagement`

**Market read:**
- Full accounting/ERP suites already ship GRN-to-GL posting as a built-in module: ERPNext
  (perpetual inventory valuation posts to GL automatically — [frappe.io](https://frappe.io/erpnext/open-source-accounting)),
  LedgerSMB ([ledgersmb.org](https://ledgersmb.org/)), Cetec ERP (posts receipts straight to an
  accrued-liability account — [cetecerp.com](https://cetecerp.com/blog/finance/goods-received-not-invoiced-grni-reconciling-accrued-liabilities/)),
  iDempiere, Axelor, PostBooks.
- On the adjacent problem — finding and clearing GRNI (goods received, not yet invoiced) —
  there's an active AI-copilot niche: Hyperbots' "Accruals Co-Pilot" discovers GRNI, books
  accruals, and reverses on invoice arrival for manufacturing/multi-plant setups
  ([hyperbots.com](https://www.hyperbots.com/copilots/accruals/accruals-discovery-for-goods-recieved)),
  and Zedly AI covers GRNI reporting/automation ([zedly.ai](https://zedly.ai/blog/goods-received-not-invoiced-report-grni-template-automation)).
- Nobody found sells the specific thing Okoh built: a small, idempotent posting *primitive*
  that guarantees one ledger effect no matter which flow created the GRN. The market solves
  this either by buying a full ERP (heavyweight) or an AI accrual-discovery copilot bolted onto
  an existing ERP (adjacent, not the same problem — discovery/aging vs. posting correctness).

**Is there a real gap?** A narrow one. Indie ERP builders and bootstrapped inventory/accounting
tools hit this exact correctness problem (double-posting when a GRN can originate two ways) and
currently either reinvent it or adopt a full suite to get it "for free." A lightweight,
well-tested reference implementation would be useful to that audience — but it's a developer
correctness pattern, not something a buyer goes looking to purchase.

**Recommendation:** Open-source, and only that — *conditional on Alpha clearing rights with
Okoh first.* This is client-engagement code; nothing here clears it for extraction. If Okoh
agrees to release a generic, de-identified version of the pattern, publishing it as a small
reference library is more likely to build reputation/inbound than to sell as IP: the addressable
buyer set for a narrow posting primitive is small, and the real audience (other builders) expects
this kind of thing to be free and inspectable, not licensed. Do not build this out into a
standalone commercial product — the ledger-posting piece alone isn't enough surface area to
charge for, and the adjacent, sellable problem (GRNI discovery/aging) is already being built by
funded competitors (Hyperbots) with AI tooling Alpha would have to match.

## 2. Bank-feed transaction matching + auto-posting engine — skip

Source: Okoh ERP (`src/lib/accounting/bank-sync/`) · `client-engagement`. Skipping a full
writeup: this space is saturated at every tier — enterprise (HighRadius, cited as a 2x Gartner
Challenger; Codat; FISPAN), SMB (QuickBooks Online's built-in auto-categorization, Acumatica Bank
Feeds), and open source (SlowBooks-Pro-2026 on GitHub, ERPNext, Finlynq, hledger+SimpleFIN all
ship bank-feed dedup and rule matching today). The category is also actively shifting from
rule-based matching (what Okoh's engine does) to AI/pattern-based matching, per industry
coverage — so a rule-based matcher is behind the current state of the art, not ahead of it. Not
worth building, open-sourcing, or selling as standalone IP. No further action; leaving it in the
backlog in case the underlying approach changes materially on a future engagement.
