# Thrones of Stories — operator guide

A separate content engine from AlphaWGA. This playbook runs the AI Bible-story
video channels that you started earlier this year and paused to focus on
AlphaWGA. Now the execution is handled by Cornelious (video editor / channel
operator) while you supervise strategy, content planning, and quality control.

## Purpose

- **Mission:** Make Bible stories visually vivid and accessible to people who would not otherwise read them.
- **Outcome:** Build a channel audience that compounds through algorithmic reach and serves as a long-tail asset.
- **Relationship to AlphaWGA:** Separate brand, separate voice, separate audience. Do not cross-post AlphaWGA content here. The only shared resource is your time and the operating discipline.

## Roles

### You (supervisor / strategist)

- Own the content calendar and series themes.
- Pick the Bible passages / stories for each period.
- Define the angle and the one-line hook for each video.
- Approve scripts and final videos before publish.
- Review analytics weekly and adjust the plan.
- Handle monetization, brand partnerships, and major decisions.

### Cornelious (operator / editor)

- Produce videos from your briefs: editing, voiceover, AI visuals, captions, thumbnails.
- Schedule and publish content according to the calendar.
- Monitor comments and community for the first 24-48 hours after posting.
- Maintain the asset library: raw footage, scripts, thumbnails, music, sound effects.
- Report weekly: what was published, what performed, what failed, what needs your input.

## Content format

### Long-form YouTube video

- Length: 3 to 8 minutes.
- Structure:
  1. Hook (0-15s): the dramatic question or tension in the story.
  2. Setup (15-60s): context, place, people.
  3. Story (60-90%): narrative arc, key scenes, dialogue.
  4. Payoff (last 30s): the lesson or question left with the viewer.
  5. CTA: like, subscribe, comment, watch the next story.
- Visual style: AI-generated scenes with consistent characters where possible.
- Voice: clear narration, subtitles burned in.

### Short-form (Reels / TikTok / Shorts)

- Length: 30 to 90 seconds.
- Structure: one scene, one tension, one payoff.
- Use clips from long-form videos where possible, but re-edit for vertical and sound-on.
- Hook must land in the first 3 seconds.

## Production workflow

1. **You plan** (Sunday): send Cornelious the coming week's briefs.
2. **Cornelious produces** (Mon-Wed): edits long-form and short-form videos.
3. **You approve** (Thu): review drafts, request edits, approve final versions.
4. **Cornelious schedules** (Fri): queue content for the next week.
5. **Publish and monitor** (daily): Cornelious publishes and monitors comments.
6. **Weekly review** (Sun): review analytics, adjust next week's plan.

## Brief template

Each brief should fit this format:

```markdown
## Story: [Name]
- Passage: [Book chapter:verse]
- Hook: [One-line tension]
- Key scenes: [3-5 bullet points]
- Visual notes: [style, characters, setting]
- CTA: [specific action for viewer]
- Target length: [short / long]
- Publish date: [YYYY-MM-DD]
- Platform: [YouTube / Reels / TikTok / Shorts]
```

## Supervision cadence

- **Weekly plan meeting**: 30 minutes, Sunday or Monday. Review last week, approve this week, resolve blockers.
- **Async review queue**: You review drafts in a shared folder or Notion/Airtable. Approval within 48 hours.
- **Monthly strategy check**: 1 hour. Review channel growth, series themes, and any pivots.

## Metrics

Track weekly once publishing begins:

| Metric | How to use it |
|--------|---------------|
| Videos published | Log what was actually shipped, not a target. Use this to calibrate Cornelious's real capacity. |
| Total views | Look for week-over-week trends, not absolute numbers. |
| Subscriber/follower growth | Platform-specific. Early phase target is consistency, not speed. |
| Average watch time / retention | >50% on shorts, >40% on long = strong quality signal. |
| Comments per video | >1% of views = healthy engagement. |

Do not set a 13-video target before the planning session. Start with what Cornelious can sustainably produce, then measure.

## Hard rules

- No AI content that misrepresents scripture. The story must match the passage.
- No clickbait that contradicts the story. The hook can be dramatic but must be honest.
- No mixing AlphaWGA and Thrones of Stories audiences unless deliberately planned.
- Cornelious cannot sign brand deals or monetization agreements without your approval.
- Every video gets your approval before publish for the first 30 videos. After that, Cornelious can publish pre-approved formats if retention stays above target.

## Open questions to confirm with Cornelious

- Which platforms are live and what are the handles?
- What AI tools does he use for visuals and voiceover?
- How many videos can he realistically produce per week?
- What is his fee structure: per video, monthly retainer, revenue share, or equity?
- Who owns the channel accounts, the email, and the monetization rights?
- What does he need from you in the brief to do his best work?

## 30-60-90 day plan

### Days 1-30: plan and pilot

- Confirm platform handles, access, and ownership with Cornelious.
- Set up shared folders, communication tools, and review workflow.
- Decide the starting cadence based on Cornelious's real capacity.
- Produce a small pilot batch (e.g., 1-2 long-form videos and 2-4 shorts) to test the workflow.
- Lock the visual style and approval process.
- Do not commit to a public posting schedule until the pilot is complete.

### Days 31-60: stabilize

- Publish on the agreed cadence.
- Identify which stories and formats get the best retention.
- Introduce a recurring series if one emerges naturally from the data.
- Set up monetization eligibility where applicable.

### Days 61-90: optimize or hold

- Decide whether to increase frequency, add a second operator, or keep the current cadence.
- Begin building a content library that can be repurposed into playlists, compilations, or a course.
- Consider a newsletter or community tie-in only if audience size warrants it.
