## Deal 1: Christian Okoh — ERP productization partnership

### Summary

- **Structure:** NewCo owned 60% by Christian Okoh (commercial partner), 40% by Bamidele Ajibola (technical partner).
- **Okoh contributes:** ₦6,000,000 development funding, industry network, distribution, 4-5 pilot companies.
- **Bamidele contributes:** ₦4,000,000 in below-market development services ("boarding"), full design and development of the multi-tenant ERP, ongoing technical leadership.
- **IP:** Assigned to NewCo upon incorporation. Until then, held per the Development Agreement.
- **Timeline:** Six months, July 2026 to December 2026, with phased deliverables.

### Key questions before signing

1. **How does Bamidele get paid for living expenses during the six-month build?**
   The ₦6M is described as "development funding," not personal salary. If it all goes to product costs, Bamidele still needs income to survive. This must be clarified before signing.

2. **What does the ₦6M actually cover?**
   Is it Okoh's capital contribution to NewCo? Is it a loan? Is it a prepayment for development? Who controls the bank account?

3. **What is the path from 40% equity to personal cash?**
   Equity is not cash. If NewCo does not pay dividends or salary, the 40% stake does not buy groceries.

4. **What happens if the 4-5 pilots do not convert to 100 users?**
   The agreement says "best efforts toward 100 users." That is not a commitment. What happens if growth stalls?

5. **Who signs on behalf of NewCo before it exists?**
   The contract says IP is held per the Development Agreement until incorporation. Need to confirm the legal mechanism.

6. **Vesting?** Is the 40% equity fully granted on day one, or does it vest over the six-month development period?

### Risk level

Medium-high. The deal is good strategically but may not solve the immediate cash problem. Do not sign until personal compensation is clarified.
