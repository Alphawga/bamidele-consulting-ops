# Implications from 2026-07-19 YouTube ingest — proposed updates to skills and playbooks

Source: ingestion of eight Daniel Priestley videos (see `2026-07-19-youtube-ingest.md`).

This note lists concrete proposed changes to business-domain files. These are
proposals, not applied edits. Each item explains why it matters and what would
change.

---

## 1. `domains/business/playbooks/offer-playbook.md`

### Why it matters
The offer ladder already aligns with Priestley's framework (gift, product for
prospects, core offer, product for clients), but the new material adds specific
operational layers: gold/silver/bronze packaging, visual aids, LAPS dashboard,
and follow-up sequences.

### Proposed changes
- Add a section on **gold, silver, and bronze** versions of the core offer.
  - Gold: over-the-top, full transformation, highest price, healthy margin.
  - Silver: middle tier, fastest path to the outcome with a few premium frills.
  - Bronze: essentials that still achieve the outcome, lowest price, healthy margin.
- Add a **visual aids requirement**: every tier must have a slide deck or brochure.
  Quote from Priestley: "If people can't see it, they're very unlikely to buy it."
- Add a **LAPS dashboard** section: weekly targets for Leads, Appointments,
  Presentations, Sales. Example starting target: 50 leads → 10 appointments →
  6 presentations → 2 sales.
- Add a **48-hour follow-up and seven-touch sequence** after every diagnostic and audit.
- Add a **reactivation campaign** template for cold leads: "Have you given up on
  [consolidated operations]?"
- Add **product-market fit validation**: 30 one-to-one sales meetings with the new
  offer packages, then the 90-day "would you be disappointed if we took it away?"
  test.

## 2. `domains/business/playbooks/operator-guide.md`

### Why it matters
The operator guide describes how to run the business domain. Priestley's "7 unsexy
habits" and the zero-to-seven-figures video add specific operating rhythms that
are not yet documented.

### Proposed changes
- Add a **perfect repeatable week** section: hour-by-hour plan for the team, tied to
  $25,000/week revenue math. Include the future-state org chart.
- Add a **Monday morning / Friday afternoon meeting** ritual with the "3 to 6 things"
  list and "locking antlers" (creative conflict) norm.
- Add a **weekly introduction workshop** fixture: a live 30-60 minute session that
  introduces the AlphaWGA methodology, delivered at least 50 times before any
  automation.
- Add a **quarterly planning retreat** ritual: one full day every 90 days focused on
  marketing materials and offer documents.
- Add an **environment / mentorship** section: spend time weekly with people 5-10x
  ahead, join an accelerator or peer group, and protect energy by blocking negative
  algorithms.

## 3. `domains/business/SKILL.md`

### Why it matters
SKILL.md defines the business domain persona, voice, and stack. The Priestley
videos suggest positioning and messaging refinements that should be deliberate,
not accidental.

### Proposed changes
- Add a **social pitch** subsection under the consulting brand or voice rules:
  "I consolidate scattered business operations into one intelligent system" as the
  name/same/fame/pain/aim/game pitch. Include a 30-45 second version.
- Add a **SALT profile** checklist (Social, Awards, Live events, Third-party platforms)
  as a high-level brand-building target, separate from the content pipeline.
- Add a note that **product-market fit precedes scale** and that the brand's
  offers should be validated with the 30-meeting / 90-day test before heavy
  go-to-market spending.
- Clarify that the voice rules apply to **published content**, but internal sales
  and operator notes should use Priestley's frameworks (e.g., LAPS, insights golf
  bag) where useful.

## 4. `domains/business/knowledge/CLAUDE.md` (content constitution)

### Why it matters
This file governs content work. The videos add a strong content cadence and
quality philosophy that may need to be explicit.

### Proposed changes
- Add a **content cadence rule**: daily short-form posts on at least one platform to
  start; expand to three or four as the business grows. The 11-views-in-90-days rule
  means silence resets visibility.
- Add a **long-form content target**: build up to four pieces of long-form content
  within 12 months (videos, podcasts, reports, or a book).
- Add a **prolific beats perfect** note: direction and volume beat polish; failures
  and mediocre posts are just data and lead-up to success.
- Add an **annual big message** rule: all content should reinforce the core line
  "I consolidate scattered business operations into one intelligent system" in
  100 different ways throughout the year.

## 5. `domains/business/content/backlog.md` and content pipeline

### Why it matters
The content backlog is the idea inbox. The videos supply many seed ideas and a
more structured approach to the scorecard landing page.

### Proposed changes
- Add content seeds derived from the videos, e.g.:
  - "The five waiting-list questions every Nigerian SME owner should answer."
  - "Why I don't sell my time for money anymore."
  - "The 30 sales meetings that prove product-market fit."
  - "Your operations are scattered if you can't answer these three questions."
  - "The 7 unsexy habits behind a seven-figure service business."
- Add a scorecard-landing-page audit task to the backlog, testing the headline
  against Priestley's frustration and results hooks.

## 6. `domains/business/playbooks/tool-ideas/backlog.md`

### Why it matters
The "stop selling time for money" video argues that AI commoditizes execution and
that the long-term move is to productize expertise into tools or platforms.

### Proposed changes
- Add a tool idea: **AI-assisted operations audit platform** that automates the
  data gathering and reporting steps of the AlphaWGA audit. Tag: `owned`.
- Add a tool idea: **scorecard follow-up automation** that branches leads into
  diagnostic, group event, or nurture tracks based on their answers. Tag: `owned`.
