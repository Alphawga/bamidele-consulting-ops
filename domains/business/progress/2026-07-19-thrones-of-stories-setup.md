## Key design decisions

- **Separate brand.** Thrones of Stories does not mix with AlphaWGA content or audience.
- **Roles split clearly.** You own strategy, calendar, briefs, and approval.
  Cornelious owns production, scheduling, publishing, and community monitoring.
- **Approval gate.** Every video gets your approval for the first 30 videos.
  After that, pre-approved formats can be published unsupervised if quality metrics hold.
- **Weekly rhythm.** Sunday plan meeting, 48-hour async review, monthly strategy check.
- **Cadence is TBD.** The starting cadence will be decided with Cornelious in the first planning session. The playbook assumes a small pilot batch, not a fixed high-volume schedule.
