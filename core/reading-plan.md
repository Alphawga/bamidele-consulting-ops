# Reading plan

A structured reading list designed to upgrade the operating model from freelancer to founder-owner. The plan is divided into four phases over roughly six months. Each phase builds on the one before it.

---

## Phase 1: Paradigm (months 1–2)

The goal is to change how you see the world, your market, and your own role.

1. **The Sovereign Individual** — James Dale Davidson & William Rees-Mogg
   - The worldview behind the empowered individual versus institutions.
   - Read first. It sets the frame for everything else.

2. **Zero to One** — Peter Thiel
   - Monopoly thinking. Stop competing like a freelancer; start owning like a founder.
   - Thiel's framework is the academic backbone of "three ways to get rich."

3. **The Prosperity Paradox** — Clayton Christensen
   - Market-creating innovation in poor countries.
   - Validates the Okoh thesis at a level the local market is not thinking at.

4. **Man-Computer Symbiosis** — J.C.R. Licklider (1960 paper)
   - Free online, one sitting.
   - Read the week you start this list. It is the original vision of human-AI collaboration.

---

## Phase 2: Founder knowledge (months 2–4)

The goal is to understand what building actually feels like and how to build something defensible.

1. **The Hard Thing About Hard Things** — Ben Horowitz
   - What building feels like when it is ugly.
   - Needed when Okoh V2 pressure meets SkinAI pressure.

2. **7 Powers** — Hamilton Helmer
   - The rigorous version of "how do I build something they cannot take from me."
   - Use this to think about the ERP becoming a moat, not just a project.

3. **The Mom Test** — Rob Fitzpatrick
   - Short, tactical. How to validate before building.
   - Read before validating SaaS #2. It will save a year.

4. **Titan** — Ron Chernow
   - Rockefeller. Infrastructure, ownership, compounding.
   - Study how an operator became the system.

---

## Phase 3: Building where you are (months 4–6)

The goal is to ground the founder mindset in the Nigerian/African context.

1. **Africa Rise and Shine** — Jim Ovia
   - Built Zenith Bank in the exact environment you are building in.
   - Your context, your constraints, proven at scale.

2. **Leapfrog** — various authors on leapfrogging infrastructure
   - Why Africa wins AI infrastructure.
   - Read while positioning SkinAI and the automation course.

3. **Elon Musk** — Walter Isaacson
   - Not for worship. For watching how responsibility, capital risk, and execution get dimensioned.
   - Useful for the co-founder equity conversations with Okoh and Nonso.

---

## Phase 4: Money and offers (ongoing)

The goal is to turn knowledge into leverage, pricing, and continuity.

1. **BOSS Moves** — Myron Golden
   - Offer, lead generation, premium pricing, continuity.
   - Your ₦500K–₦3M engagements and the retainer structure live inside these four moves.

2. **The Almanack of Naval Ravikant** — Eric Jorgenson
   - Equity, leverage, and specific knowledge.
   - You have the specific knowledge; this teaches you to price it.

---

## Reading execution

- Read in the morning when possible, before the day's noise starts.
- One book at a time. Finish the phase before moving to the next.
- Distill each book into a note in `domains/business/knowledge/` when done, or `domains/book-writing/knowledge/` if the topic is more about writing/identity.
- Track daily progress in `core/daily/YYYY-MM-DD.md` under the **Read** non-negotiable.
